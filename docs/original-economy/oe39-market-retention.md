## Separate versioned contract

**M Needs-aware logistics** in the menu, or `--original-market-logistics` with
City-v16, explicitly creates Rule13. Existing O/F/S choices retain Rules10/11/12.
General City-v11 Rule3 and ordinary City-v16 Rule4 defaults remain unchanged.
Schema29 requires the immutable marker:

```json
"market_logistics": "authored-needs-aware-market-v1"
```

It belongs under `rules`, identifies this authored policy and introduces no saved
demand cache, cursor, actor or accounting authority. Exact-generation create/restore,
F5/F9, menu load and Recovery preserve the selected rule. Old schema28 files are
not migrated or accepted merely by changing their rule/schema headers. The original
model, map and Difficulty fingerprints and all existing geometry, actor, transfer,
conservation, finance and fire-provenance checks remain required before publication.

Only Warehouse-to-Market/Paper and Market-to-House commodity selection changes:

- Actual missing current goods precede current buffers, then usable next-stage
  goods. The query uses real House stock plus committed incoming units. A buffer
  at or below four can be refilled within the unchanged eight-unit capacity;
  a missing supported current need takes precedence even above that threshold.
- A next-stage request needs a real evolution target in the same House family,
  actual DES and met non-goods requirements. Common10 is terminal. Bronze/Lacquer
  alternatives account for actual stock and incoming commitments.
- A Warehouse considers the same needs among households reachable from a staffed
  paid Market's distributor, subtracting the Market pool and its incoming goods.
  The staffed Paper input chain remains available without granting Paper or visits.
- Within a priority tier, normalized available stock per need is compared, then
  the existing saved `(target, good)` cursor breaks ties deterministically. No
  House ID receives a special priority.

The pure policy selects a candidate; World still owns reservation, loading, actual
movement, arrival, cancellation and physical return. Source stock, target headroom,
operation, fire, workforce and existing revisioned route gates continue to apply.
The selector uses bounded existing bodies/House rows and cached routes; it adds no
World copy, BFS, map scan or frame work. See [architecture](architecture-map.md)
for the full authority boundary.

Rule13 inherits Rule12's prices, model values, production/harvest/consumption,
immigration, services, DES, taxes, two Hemp chains, three Food chains, FireWatch/
Inspector behavior and installed upkeep of2 Funds per required worker per816 ticks.
House stock remains8 per good, cargo4 and travel5 movement ticks per edge. Global
82-body/58-courier/20-House bounds are unchanged. No extra actor, Farm cap, cargo
capacity or resource grant is introduced. This is authored OpenEmperor dispatch;
no original-EXE claim follows from its effectiveness.
